Three matches to win him over

How do you play the stroke?
Every shot has two steps

1. Timing. Tap the court just as the ring closes on the ball. The closer to perfect, the bigger your sweet spot.
2. Stroke. The view cuts to your racket and the ball, on your right or your left: play a forehand or a backhand to match. The wrong one swings at thin air. Low to high is topspin, level is flat, high to low is slice. Harder is faster.

She runs to the ball by herself. With swipes, the direction of your swipe is where the ball goes. Swipe on the side where the ball is: the wrong side is a miss. Keys: Space to tap, then Q A Z for the left side and E D C for the right side (topspin, flat, slice).

📱 Real racket swing

Hold the phone like a racket handle (or tape it flat to a racket). Tap the screen in time, then swing a real stroke: the motion sensors read forehand or backhand, topspin, flat or slice, and how hard.

Best with this screen mirrored to a TV, so you can watch while you swing.
